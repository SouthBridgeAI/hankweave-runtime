## The Key Insight About Sequential Reliability

What if we lean into the fact that phases are like **proof steps** in mathematics? Each phase proves something works, and the next phase can rely on that proof. This is why:
- Only one can run at a time (you prove things sequentially)
- Chroniclers can't interfere (they're reading the proof, not writing it)
- Workspaces provide **lemmas** (proven sub-routines you can invoke)
- The whole thing ossifies into a **theorem** (a reliable, proven process)

This might be too academic, but it captures the rigor.
